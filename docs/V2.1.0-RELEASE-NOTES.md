# RaceLab v2.1.0

RaceLab v2.1.0 adds the F1 Telemetry Lab: a read-only workspace for studying
the laps of a completed F1 25 session.

- **Lap traces (M1):** the Lab reads a bounded, downsampled trace of a recorded
  lap on demand: speed, RPM, throttle, brake, steering, gear, lap time and lap
  distance. Raw frames never leave the backend, and recordings are never
  modified.
- **Telemetry Lab workspace (M2):** open a completed F1 session from Sessions.
  A current and a reference lap share one cursor across stacked charts, speed
  and RPM gauges and steering readouts, on a distance or time axis. What
  RaceLab does not record (track geometry, steering angle, tyre and fuel
  detail) is stated, never drawn or estimated.
- **Replay (M3):** play, pause, seek and change rate (0.5×–4×). Every chart,
  gauge and readout follows the same lap position.
- **Lap analysis and quality (M4):** each lap shows its timing source (Session
  History or provisional Lap Data), whether the game counted it, and its
  sector times. Sample coverage is measured from the recorded 10 Hz samples.
  A lap counts as ready for distance-aligned analysis only when its timing is
  confirmed and valid and its time and distance samples cover the whole lap
  without breaks. Anything that rules a lap out is listed.

**Verification:** the Telemetry Lab has been verified with deterministic
synthetic fixtures, automated frontend and Rust tests, and real-browser layout
captures at 1920, 1280 and 960 px. It has **not yet been validated with an
actual F1 25 recording.**

**Not included:** lap delta time, further lap-comparison work (planned M5 and
M6) and a track map. The delta panel states whether two laps would qualify,
but draws no delta. RaceLab records no F1 world position, so no circuit is
drawn.

RaceLab runs locally without accounts or telemetry uploads. The Windows x64
package uses a per-user NSIS installer.
