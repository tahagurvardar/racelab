# RaceLab v2.2.0

RaceLab v2.2.0 extends the F1 Telemetry Lab with lap comparison and a delta
time measured from recorded lap times. Both run only between two laps that
the v2.1 lap-quality checks (M4) accept: confirmed by Session History, valid,
and with time and distance samples covering the whole lap without breaks.

- **Lap comparison (M5):** on Distance sync, the current lap is compared with
  the reference at the same lap distance for speed, throttle, brake and
  steering. Each chart shows the current-minus-reference difference at the
  cursor, and the Lap comparison card lists the mean and largest difference
  per channel; selecting the largest moves the cursor there. Each lap is read
  at its own recorded values: nothing is interpolated, carried across a
  telemetry gap or filled in, and a channel recorded over too little of the
  shared distance reports why instead of showing figures.
- **Delta time (M6):** the Delta panel draws the current lap's elapsed time
  minus the reference's at the same lap distance; above zero the current lap
  is behind. Elapsed time comes only from each lap's recorded lap time and lap
  distance readings, linear only between adjacent readings at most 300 ms
  apart. There is no delta across a longer step or a telemetry gap, before a
  lap's first reading or after its last, and speed is never converted into
  time. A lap that runs backwards, whose time does not rise with its
  distance, or a delta measured over less than 95% of the shared distance
  shows a reason instead of a number. The readout follows the shared cursor
  and replay, and the Lap comparison card states the final and largest delta.
- **Chart labels:** every Lab chart's value labels now line up with their
  gridlines.

On Time sync both laps are still overlaid on their own clocks, but neither
the comparison nor the delta is computed, since the same time is not the
same point of the circuit.

**Verification:** M5 and M6 have been verified with deterministic synthetic
fixtures, automated frontend and Rust tests, and real-browser layout captures
at 1920, 1280 and 960 px. On the synthetic laps the final delta equals the
difference of the two recorded lap times. They have **not yet been validated
with an actual F1 25 recording**, and the v2.2.0 installer has **not been
tested manually**.

**Not included:** a track map (RaceLab records no F1 world position), tyre
and fuel detail in the Lab, and comparison or delta on Time sync.

RaceLab runs locally without accounts or telemetry uploads. The Windows x64
package uses a per-user NSIS installer.
